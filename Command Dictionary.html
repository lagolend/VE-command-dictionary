Vote Commands

/votelist

Shows information regarding the rewards earned by voting and provides the to an overview of all
6 websites you can vote on. These websites contain a leaderboard of Minecraft servers, and Vanilla Europa
encourages its players to vote for them by rewardin them for doing so. Each vote earns you 1 Vote Crate key
(More information under Crate Commands) and gets you closer to the next vote rank.
(For more information on ranks go to Rank Commands or /voteranks under Vote Commands.)
Voting on all sites in a day earns you 500 claim blocks. (More information under Claim Commands.)
Please take note that even though 6 websites are provided, only 5 are needed to be voted on to count as
"all websites". It's that way in case one of the websites malfunctions, you can still vote on "all" of them.
600 votes earns you a Special Crate key. Voting on all websites for 30 days in a row also earns you the Special Crate key
(More information under Crate Commands) Each vote enters you into the VoteParty
(For more information on the VoteParty go to Crate Commands or /voteparty under Vote Commands.)

/crazycrates

Opens the Crates Menu.
Vanilla Europa uses the to make crates possible on the server.
A crate is opened by using the corresponding crate key. There are 4 Crate types on VE.
The Vote Crate, keys to which are obtained by voting (More information under Vote Commands).
The Vote Crate contains various vanilla and non-vanilla minecraft items along with a Tags
Crate key and a Special Crate key. these are added to your key-balance without ever
showing up in your inventory, so dont be confused by that.
The Tags Crate contains small images that you can display after your in game name.
(More information under Cosmetic Commands) Keys are obtained from Vote Crates and VoteParty Crates.
By opening the Special Crate you can get rare valuable items. The key is rather hard to obtain.
The possible ways to get one are: Buy one from the , get a Special Crate key from the
Vote Crate, get two Special Crate keys from the Special Crate, get a Special Crate key from the
VoteParty Crate or vote for 30 days in a row. Every 600 votes you are also rewardet a Special Crate key.
The VoteParty Crate has medium valuable loot. VoteParty happens every time we collectivelly reach 3500 votes.
If you have contributed at least 12 votes to the VoteParty and are online when the goal is reached,
you are given a VoteParty Crate key.

/mass-open-votes

Opens multiple Vote Crates at once to save you time. Your loot will be documented in chat.
Only you can see it. I have estimated that it opens 11-13 vote crates at once, or as many as fit
in your inventory (if you cant fit all 13). This is my DIY estimate, I couldn't find any reliable information
regarding the amount. 11-13 seem like awfully ugly numbers to be true. I also doubt that you get keys
from the Vote Crate when using this command, but due to the fact that its randomized I can't prove it.
There is a non zero chance I'm just very unlucky. I assume that /mass-open-votes is a little wierd
due to the fact that it was only recntly added.
